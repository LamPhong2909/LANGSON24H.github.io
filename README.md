   <section id="intro">
            <h2>🚀 Project Introduction</h2>
            <p><strong>LANGSON24H</strong> is a strategic information portal and data dashboard serving the "Optimization of Mineral Exploitation and Economic Development of Lang Son" project.</p>
            
   <div class="highlight">
                <p>This project serves as a <strong>Digital Gateway</strong> between:</p>
                <ol>
                    <li><strong>Government:</strong> Manage planning, monitor revenue, and enforce ESG/ISO 14001 standards.</li>
                    <li><strong>Investors (FDI/PPP):</strong> Access transparent data on high-quality limestone reserves (CaCO3 > 98.5%) and incentive mechanisms (Resolution 19/2024/NQ-HĐND).</li>
                    <li><strong>Community:</strong> Monitor environmental impact and socio-economic effectiveness.</li>
                </ol>
            </div>
        </section>

  <section id="context">
            <h2>📉 Strategic Context & Challenges</h2>
            <p>Based on in-depth analysis reports, Lang Son faces a "middle-income trap" in the mineral industry:</p>
            
   <h3>Current Situation:</h3>
            <p>Export of raw materials (Clinker, construction stone) with low value ($31-$35/ton). Negative environmental impact and waste of land resources.</p>
            
   <h3>Challenges:</h3>
            <ul>
                <li>High capital barriers (CAPEX) for advanced technology up to $30-45 million USD.</li>
                <li>Shortage of high-tech human resources (Material Chemistry, Jet Mill operation).</li>
                <li>Outdated technology, unable to separate MgO and Fe2O3 impurities.</li>
            </ul>
            
   <h3>Opportunity:</h3>
            <p>Transition to <strong>Nano PCC</strong> and <strong>Coated GCC</strong> can increase value to <strong>$350-$500/ton (10-15 times higher)</strong>.</p>
        </section>

   <section id="solution">
            <h2>💻 Technology Solution & Features</h2>
            <p>The LANGSON24H project is built to solve the above challenges through modern web technology:</p>
            
   <h3>1. "Geo-Economic" Dashboard</h3>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Visualizer:</strong> Interactive real-time economic value comparison charts between Clinker vs. Nano PCC.</li>
                <li><strong>Mining Map:</strong> Digital map of key mineral mines in Huu Lung, Chi Lang with high-purity CaCO3 reserves (>98.5%) and low impurity content (MgO <= 0.1%).</li>
            </ul>
            
   <h3>2. Investment Promotion Portal</h3>
            <ul>
                <li>Integrated ROI calculation tools for BOO/PPP models.</li>
                <li>Transparent system for licensing procedures and incentives according to Resolution 19/2024.</li>
                <li>Showcase of potential projects: PCC Nano Factory, Coated GCC Production Line, Geo-polymer Factory from waste sludge.</li>
            </ul>
            
   <h3>3. ESG & Circular Economy Monitoring System</h3>
            <ul>
                <li>Tracking carbon emission indicators and waste sludge/tailings reuse rates.</li>
                <li>Green certification management module (LEED, GREENMARK, ISO 14001) for participating businesses.</li>
            </ul>
        </section>
